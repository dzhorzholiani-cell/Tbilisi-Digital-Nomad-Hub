1% Tax Guide (Individual Entrepreneur)

Georgia offers a 1% flat tax for turnover under 500,000 GEL.

  1. Register as an Individual Entrepreneur at the Public Service Hall.
  2. Apply for Small Business Status on the RS.GE portal.
  3. Status activation is immediate upon registration.
